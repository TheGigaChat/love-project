# Visual references

Each month can have its own typography, palette, layout, and motion. Use these examples to pick an approach that suits the memories already collected for that month.

## Russian typography

- **Lora** gives the cover and first chapter a warm serif with a distinct italic. [Cyreal type specimen](https://cyrealtype.github.io/Lora-Cyrillic/)
- **Golos Text** works for body copy and playback controls. Its heaviest weight gives the second chapter a bolder entrance. [Google Fonts specimen](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Golos+Text)
- **PT Serif** suits letters and longer passages. [ParaType specimen](https://info.paratype.ru/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/ptserif55reg.pdf)
- **Prata** has the sharp contrast for a sparse, formal chapter title. [Google Fonts specimen](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Prata)
- **Oranienbaum** suits a photo-led chapter with a large, classic headline. [Google Fonts specimen](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Oranienbaum)
- **Old Standard TT** suits dates and captions alongside archival images. [Google Fonts specimen](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Old+Standard+TT)
- **Yeseva One** gives a short title a more playful silhouette. [Google Fonts specimen](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Yeseva+One)
- **Manrope** offers a geometric sans serif for a contemporary chapter or small labels. [Designer’s specimen](https://www.sharanda.com/manrope)

## Phone layouts and relationship stories

- **Memory Journal by DIGI.CO** shows a full-screen portrait with a short note and a small music control. Start with the middle phone mockup for a dark, portrait-first chapter. [Dribbble concept](https://dribbble.com/shots/27613786-Memory-Journal-Mobile-App-Design)
- **Anil & Eswari** puts dated memories and real photographs in order. Its Our Love Story section is a reference for chronology and short captions. [Live story](https://anil-eswari.netlify.app/)
- **Museum of Us** frames ordinary photos and messages like personal exhibits. Try one month as a set of framed objects with small labels and a tap to reveal the memory. [Figma Community file](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1603538689877194356/museum-of-us-interactive-valentines-day-card)
- **Relive by Luca Canu** places text cards inside a stream of images. Useful for breaking up a regular photo grid and giving one memory more space. [Dribbble concept](https://dribbble.com/shots/24595032-relive-Mobile-App-Design-Concept)
- **Lumière by Kawsar Ahmed** is a tactile mobile scrapbook concept with soft shadows and overlapping layers. [Dribbble concept](https://dribbble.com/shots/27457582-Lumi-re-A-digital-scrapbook)
- **Core Memories** uses scrapbook details, editorial type, and photo-led compositions across a wedding concept. [Dribbble concept](https://dribbble.com/shots/27895453-Core-Memories-Wedding-Experience-Website-UI-UX-Design)
- **Romantic Portrait Wedding Invitation** uses one full-bleed portrait with large serif text. A reference for an opening that leaves room for a single Start button. [Figma Community file](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1496548400667387323/romantic-portrait-wedding-invitation)

## Chapter moods and composition

- **Greetings from Seoul by Vilda Witaria** mixes phone photos, postcard-like clusters, dark gutters, and burnt-orange accents. Look at how it alternates one big image with several smaller ones. [Behance zine](https://www.behance.net/gallery/199677695/zine-greetings-from-seoul-autumn-2023)
- **In the Mood for Love by Jia Jia and CK** uses close crops, deep shadows, and red-orange light. A reference for a passionate or bittersweet sequence. [Behance concept album](https://www.behance.net/gallery/96480595/inthemoodforlove-conceptual-album-design)
- **Book of Correspondence by Maria Frunchak** combines muted blue, paper texture, and an envelope-like cover. A reference for the opening or a quiet section after the final month. [Behance book design](https://www.behance.net/gallery/254630523/Book-of-Correspondence-design)
- **Negatives Flag Book** turns film and contact prints into an accordion sequence. Imagine a pinned scene where a vertical swipe moves the strip sideways before one frame fills the screen. [Behance book design](https://www.behance.net/gallery/13946945/Negatives-Flag-Book)
- **Paper Cut Mixed Media Collage Exploration** layers irregular hand-cut type and photography. Good for a playful title that assembles from shapes or a photo that slides between paper pieces. [Behance collage studies](https://www.behance.net/gallery/233727809/Paper-Cut-Mixed-Media-Collage-Exploration)
- **Photo Diary, 134 Exposures** alternates color, grayscale, and dark negative sheets. Useful for sparse photo arrangements, uneven panels, and pauses between images. [Behance photo diary](https://www.behance.net/gallery/228322371/Photo-Diary-134-Exposures)
- **FOMU** uses solid and outlined typography to suggest content and a frame. Pair it with Museum of Us for a chapter styled as a photo exhibition. [Behance identity](https://www.behance.net/gallery/51743829/FOMU)
- **Triennale Milano** uses a foldout format, bright blocks, rotated type, and a route with stops. A reference for a trip, a walk, or a chapter built around places. [Behance zine](https://www.behance.net/gallery/227388815/Triennale-Milano-Interactive-Folded-Zine-Design)
- **Posting Letters** documents a long-distance relationship through postcards made from letters spotted in two cities. A reference for turning everyday details from the year into chapter typography or artifacts. [Behance project](https://www.behance.net/gallery/50438431/Posting-Letters)
- **35mm Gallery by Pedro Rogério** treats a digital collection like printed photographs and developing negatives. A reference for film textures and photos that can be handled. [Behance project](https://www.behance.net/gallery/234013599/35mm-Gallery)

## Live stories and motion

- **bleibtgleich’26 by Maksym Ponomarenko** is a reference for soft-focus type, text that comes into view, and image masks with shaped edges. [Live site](https://bleibtgleich.dev/) · [Creator’s breakdown](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/09/23/bleibtgleich26-a-180-turn-from-brutalism-to-minimalism/)
- **Scroll Storytelling** shows text coming into focus, pieces appearing on a dark canvas, and a mask growing to reveal an image. Study its Text and Mask scenes. [Live demo](https://scroll-storytelling-bay.vercel.app/) · [Project source](https://github.com/decriptcypher/scroll-storytelling)
- **The Boat by SBS** is a playback reference for manual scroll, auto-scroll, sound, and chapter navigation. Its sound team describes giving individual moments room to breathe. [Live story](https://www.sbs.com.au/theboat/) · [SBS sound design notes](https://www.sbs.com.au/whats-on/article/the-sound-and-vision-of-the-boat/67xsuhjh5)
- **Welcome to Pine Point** uses photos, video, and ephemera like a family album. The live intro failed in the original research browser, so start with the trailer. [Creator’s trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qSHPe_dbGtU) · [NFB interview](https://blog.nfb.ca/blog/2011/02/03/welcome-to-pine-point-an-interview-with-the-goggles/)
- **The Ring Journey** shows a video scene moving forward and backward with scroll as text comes into view. A reference for a continuous transition between scenes. [Live demo](https://wanjadoering.de/projekte/ring-reise/demo-en/) · [Creator’s breakdown](https://wanjadoering.de/en/projects/06_ring-reise/)
- **MERSI by FLOT NOIR** shows how staggered editorial compositions can resolve into a grid and how a project can move sideways through images. [Case study with visual examples](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/07/27/between-print-and-digital-the-making-of-mersis-website/)
- **Firestorm** is an earlier example of a chapter-based story built from family photographs, short text, and video. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/interactive/2013/may/26/firestorm-bushfire-dunalley-holmes-family)
